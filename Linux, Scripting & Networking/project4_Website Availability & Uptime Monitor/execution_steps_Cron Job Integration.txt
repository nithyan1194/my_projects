Execution & Verification

Step 1: Grant Execution Privileges

chmod +x uptime_monitor.sh

Step 2: Run the Availability Probe

./uptime_monitor.sh targets.txt

Step 3: Inspect the Telemetry Ledger

cat uptime_audit.log

Scheduled Monitoring (Cron Job Integration)
To run this health check every 5 minutes automatically, add this entry to your crontab (crontab -e):

*/5 * * * * /bin/bash /path/to/uptime_monitor.sh /path/to/targets.txt > /dev/null 2>&1
